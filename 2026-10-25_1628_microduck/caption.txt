Want to train a real robot, not just a chatbot? Meet Microduck from Pollen Robotics.

A 25 cm, 800 g biped with 15 motors. Out of the box it walks, kicks a ball, picks things up, gets back up after a fall, and rolls on skates. The SDK, simulation and full RL training stack are open source (Apache-2.0): train new moves in MuJoCo, then run them on the robot. $399 introductory price before taxes and shipping, on pre-order. Training your own policies needs a CUDA GPU (or Hugging Face Jobs).
