Paper Collage

Cut paper and torn tags. Good for maps, places and "why is…" questions.

From the videoSTYLESFABLE
Make a 6-second vertical animation (1080x1920) as one HTML file I can play. Explain why world maps are wrong as a paper photo-collage. Everything looks cut from printed paper, with halftone dots, a white sticker edge and a soft shadow. Put a big serif headline behind one large cut-out subject and add 3 torn paper tags with short facts. Animate the one thing the fact is about, with a stop-motion wobble. Real facts only.

Flat Science

Glowing shapes on dark purple. Good for the body, chemistry, how things work.

From the videoSTYLESFABLE
Make a 6-second vertical animation (1080x1920) as one HTML file I can play. Explain why coffee wakes you up in a flat, glowing vector style on a deep purple background. Use simple rounded shapes with a soft glow, mint, pink and yellow accents, and small pill labels. Show cause and effect in 3 steps, with light travelling from one step to the next and a label popping up at each. Real facts only.

Painted Story

A hand-painted storybook look. Good for history and big events.

From the videoSTYLESFABLE

Start close on a detail, show the key moment, then slowly pull back to the whole scene. Add short captions on torn paper strips. Historical facts only.

What If

A 3D "what would happen" scene.

From the videoEARTHSPACECOSMOS
Make a 6-second vertical 3D animation (1080x1920) as one HTML file with three.js. Show what would happen if Earth stopped spinning. The ground stops, but everything on it keeps moving east. Use stylised 3D shapes with soft materials, one strong light and real shadows. Keep the camera low and cinematic, and put one bold caption at the top with the key fact in a highlight box. Show the facts happening, don't just state them. Real facts only.
