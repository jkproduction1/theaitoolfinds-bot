# 5 animation styles (prompts for Claude Code) — DM for comment STYLES

Paste into Claude Code (or save each as a slash command in .claude/commands/<name>.md). Swap the [brackets] for your topic.
All are 9:16, ~3–6 s, HTML + GSAP, rendered frame by frame.

/ink-wash — Make a 9:16 animation in the style of a Chinese ink-wash painting about [topic]. Cream rice-paper background with grain, 4 layered mountain ridges in soft grey ink (back layers lighter and blurrier), a red sun slowly rising, drifting mist, a tiny boat crossing the water, two birds, vertical serif title letter by letter and a red seal stamp at the end. Slow, quiet, parallax.

/headline — Make a 9:16 animation of a printed newspaper story about [topic]. A cream newspaper page with a serif masthead, date line, red kicker, big serif headline, byline and a justified body with a drop cap, slightly rotated on a dark warm background. The camera starts close and settles out; then a lime highlighter sweeps over the two key sentences, one after the other.

/stat-chart — Make a 9:16 fintech stat animation for [metric]. Near-black background, small brand row, label, a huge counter that counts up, a line chart that draws itself grey before the turning point and orange after it, a dot and dashed marker at the turning point with a 2-line annotation, month labels, an orange tag at the end. Mark it "sample data" if the numbers aren't real.

/teardown — Make a 9:16 exploded-view teardown of [product]. Dark gradient background, isometric 3D (CSS 3D or three.js). Start with the closed product, then separate it into 5 layers that float apart vertically with expo easing; list the numbered parts below (name + one short spec each) appearing one by one.

/receipt — Make a 9:16 animation of a thermal receipt that prints [your story / list]. A black printer with a green LED at the top, white paper feeding out line by line in steps, monospace text: header, order and date, line items, double rule, a big TOTAL line, barcode, "thank you" and a zigzag torn edge; a small drop when it finishes.
