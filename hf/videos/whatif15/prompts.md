# whatif15 prompts — DM for comment WHATIF
Save as Claude Code commands (.claude/commands/<name>.md, "$ARGUMENTS" = your topic) or paste directly.

/zack-d-films — Make a 9:16 full-screen 3D "what happens if" short (three.js, rendered frame by frame with HTML + GSAP, ~4 s) of $ARGUMENTS:
one clear dramatic shot, a simple character, real physics, labels with leader lines, a short caption with one key word in a box.
  Example: /zack-d-films jumping into a hole through Earth  (Earth cut in half, you speed up to the centre, slow down, pop out
  on the other side: ~38 min with Earth's real density)

/what-if — 3D what-if of $ARGUMENTS: start with the normal situation, then the change, then what happens next, true to real physics.
  Example: /what-if the Sun disappeared  (8 min 20 s of light still on its way, then darkness and Earth flies off in a straight line)

/zoom-out — One continuous log-scale pull-back of $ARGUMENTS, with a label for each scale step.
  Example: /zoom-out to the edge of the universe  (your city, Earth, Solar System, Milky Way ~100,000 ly, observable universe ~93 billion ly)
