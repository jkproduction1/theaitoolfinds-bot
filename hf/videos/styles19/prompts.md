# styles19 prompts — DM for comment STYLE
Save as Claude Code commands (.claude/commands/<name>.md, "$ARGUMENTS" = your topic) or paste directly.
All three: 9:16, HTML canvas + GSAP, rendered frame by frame, ~6 s, your own drawings only (no logos, mascots or characters of any channel).

/paper-explainer — Explain $ARGUMENTS as a full-screen paper photo-collage: layered depth (sky, far and near landscape, a blurred
foreground), one big cut-out subject with a white sticker edge, drop shadow and print halftone, a huge serif headline that sits
BEHIND the cut-out, one orange label, a hand-drawn marker arrow, then 3 torn cream paper tags (taped on, yellow highlighter on the
key word) that each point at a part of the subject. Paper grain over everything, slow parallax, stop-motion wobble at 12 fps.
  Example: /paper-explainer why flights cost so much  (plane over mountains → Fuel, Airport fees, Demand)

/flat-cosmos — Explain $ARGUMENTS as a flat vector animation on a deep blue-purple background: simple rounded shapes in 3 flat
tones each, soft glow on everything that is alive or active, blurred shapes drifting in the background for depth, tiny floating
particles, a slow camera push-in, pill labels with a thin leader line, a bold white headline with one coloured word.
  Example: /flat-cosmos how your body fights a virus  (virus docks on a cell → a white blood cell engulfs and digests it →
  antibodies tag the others)

/storybook — Tell $ARGUMENTS as a hand-painted storybook scene: warm limited palette, visible brush strokes and canvas grain,
light "boil" (texture changes at 8 fps), simple silhouette figures with long shadows, short captions on torn parchment strips,
start close on the action and end with a slow pull-back reveal and one glint of light.
  Example: /storybook how the pyramids were built  (workers drag a block on a sled over wet sand → a long ramp → the pyramid
  rises course by course → golden capstone)
