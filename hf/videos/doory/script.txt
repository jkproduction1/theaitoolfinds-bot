Paying for Canva Pro just to make posts and thumbnails? There's a free, open source one called Doory. It's a design editor that runs in your browser. Posters, stories, slides, thumbnails, even resumes. Edit the text, drag in shapes and icons, and remove a background right in the browser. Add animations, page transitions and music, then export as MP4 or GIF. No paid plan, no locked features, no ads. You host it yourself with Docker, and it's still early, with ten templates so far.
