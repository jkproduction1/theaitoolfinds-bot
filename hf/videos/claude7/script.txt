Most people use Claude Code like a chat box. These seven commands change that. One: slash B-T-W. Ask a side question without filling your context. Two: press Escape twice to roll back to an earlier checkpoint. Three: slash compact. It summarizes the chat to free up context. Four: slash context shows what's eating your context window. Five: Shift Tab switches to plan mode. Read only, no edits. Six: Control R searches your old prompts. Seven: slash simplify reviews your recent changes and fixes them.
