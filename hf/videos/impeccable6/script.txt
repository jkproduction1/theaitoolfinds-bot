Ever noticed every AI-built website looks the same? Inter font, purple gradients, cards inside cards. Impeccable gives your coding agent the words to fix it. Here are six commands. One: bolder, for designs that play it too safe. Two: quieter tones down loud ones. Three: critique scores your design and ranks what to fix first. Four: audit checks accessibility, speed and mobile layouts. Five: polish, the final pass before you ship. Six: live. Pick an element in your browser and flip through variants. Free and open source.
