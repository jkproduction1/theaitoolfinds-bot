# route3 prompts — DM for comment ANIMATE
Save as Claude Code commands (.claude/commands/<name>.md, "$ARGUMENTS" = your topic) or paste directly.

/route-map — Animated 3D route for $ARGUMENTS (three.js, 9:16, rendered frame by frame with HTML + GSAP, ~4 s): a soft white 3D city
like a nav app (blocks, parks, sidewalks), a blue route draws itself through the streets from start to destination, the camera rides
behind a pulsing blue puck, then pulls up to show the whole route and a pin drops on the destination. Labels for start and end.
  Example: /route-map airport to the hotel

/holo-scan — Blue hologram of $ARGUMENTS on a projector base: a glowing scan line rises and builds the model as wireframe + faint fill
(additive glow, bloom). Animate what flows through it; highlight the working parts in orange. Labels with leader lines. Textbook facts only.
  Example: /holo-scan a hydro dam  (reservoir stores water high → penstocks → water spins the turbines → generators make electricity)

/iso-flow — Light isometric diorama of $ARGUMENTS: a rounded slab with a road, trees, buildings; a small car drives the route, the
travelled road turns blue and numbered pins pop in at each step with white labels. Orthographic camera, soft shadows.
  Example: /iso-flow from home to the shop  (1 Home → 2 Crossroads → 3 Shop)
