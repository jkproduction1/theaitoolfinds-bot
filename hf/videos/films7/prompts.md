# films7 prompts — DM for comment FILMS
Save each as a Claude Code command (.claude/commands/<name>.md, "$ARGUMENTS" = your idea) or paste it directly.

/zack-d-films — A 6-second vertical (9:16) "what if" 3D short about $ARGUMENTS, in ONE self-contained HTML file with three.js, rendered by
a deterministic render(t). Smooth stylised 3D (soft materials, one hard key light, real shadows), a low cinematic camera that slowly
pushes in, one bold caption at the top with the key fact in a highlight box. Get the science right (textbook facts only) and SHOW it:
e.g. on the Moon gravity is about 1/6 of Earth's and there's no air, so the jump is slow and high and the dust flies in clean arcs.

/neon-silhouette — A looping 9:16 neon animation of $ARGUMENTS, one HTML file, canvas 2D. A glowing stick figure (pink core, white hot
centre, soft glow) on a dark night city: two parallax skyline layers with lit windows and neon signs, a wet street that mirrors the
figure, cyan light trails behind every moving limb. Smooth run cycle, deterministic render(t), ~2 s loop.

/ink-doodle — A 9:16 hand-drawn ink animation of $ARGUMENTS, one HTML file, canvas 2D. Cream paper with grain and a soft vignette,
black ink lines that "boil" (re-jitter) 8 times a second, one watercolour accent colour that slightly misses the lines, simple stick
figure acting with clear anticipation → action → reaction, little ink effects (rain dashes, wind swirls, a "!" over the head).
