# styles10 prompts — DM for comment CAR
One base prompt + one style line. Paste into Claude Code (or save as .claude/commands/<style>.md).

BASE: Make a 9:16 animation (~4 s, HTML canvas or three.js, rendered frame by frame with GSAP) of a red sports car driving through
a scene, side view. Draw everything in code, no images. Style:

1. Pixel RPG — draw at 112x199 and upscale with no smoothing; night city with lit pixel windows, twinkling stars, a pixel moon,
   scrolling road dashes, a tiny HUD (level + bar), the car bobs 1 px.
2. Watercolor — off-white paper with grain, soft bleeding washes (blurred irregular blobs) for sky and hills, the car painted in
   3–4 translucent offset layers with a darker pigment edge, a handwritten serif line at the top.
3. Ukiyo-e — cream woodblock paper, a big red sun, a rising Prussian-blue wave with white foam claws and flow lines, the car in flat
   colours with thick black outlines crossing an arched wooden bridge, a red vertical seal.
4. Chalkboard — dark green board with smudges and a wooden frame, the car sketched stroke by stroke in chalk (jittered double
   strokes), handwritten title, arrows for thrust, drag and weight.
5. Neon Glow — synthwave: striped gradient sun, magenta perspective grid scrolling toward you, the car as glowing cyan lines,
   pink spinning wheels, light trails behind.
6. Blueprint — blue sheet with fine and major grid, the car drawn in thin white lines, wheel centre marks, dimension arrows for
   length, wheelbase and height (no numbers), a title block in the corner.
7. Comic Pop — yellow background with orange halftone dots, a white action burst with "VROOM!", black speed lines, the car with a
   thick ink outline and a shine stripe, slight shake, thick panel border.
8. Papercut — layered paper hills with soft drop shadows and parallax, a paper sun and clouds, the car as a white paper cut-out
   with a red stripe.
9. Claymation — stepped 12 fps with a tiny wobble, soft clay shading (gradients, rounded fat outlines), finger-print marks, a clay
   tree and cloud made of balls, a soft contact shadow.
10. Low-poly PS1 — three.js at 160x284 scaled up pixelated, flat-shaded boxes and 5–6 sided cylinders, fog, low-poly trees and
    mountains, a chase camera swinging around the car on a road.
