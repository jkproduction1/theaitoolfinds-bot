This tiny robot duck learned to walk before it ever touched the floor. Microduck is 25 centimeters tall, and its brain was trained in a MuJoCo simulation, with 4096 virtual ducks at once. A usable walk takes about one to two hours on a GPU. Then the same brain runs on the real robot, 50 times a second. Knock it over and it gets back up. Put it on wheels and it loads a second brain, just for rolling. And yes, it quacks in its own voice.
