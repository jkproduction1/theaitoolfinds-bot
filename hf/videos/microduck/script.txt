Want to train a real robot, not just a chatbot? Meet Microduck from Pollen Robotics. It's a 25 centimeter biped, about 800 grams, with fifteen motors. Out of the box it walks, kicks a ball, picks things up and gets back up when you knock it over. Put on skates and it rolls. The whole training stack is open source.
