Everyone missed the point of Microduck. It's a 399 dollar robot duck you teach new skills yourself. Nobody hand-codes its walk. You train it in a simulation with thousands of virtual ducks at once, and in about two hours it can walk. Then that brain drives the real robot. Knock it over, it gets back up. Put it on wheels, it swaps to a second brain. The whole stack is open source, so the next trick is yours. 20,000 people pre-ordered it in a day. And yes, it quacks.
