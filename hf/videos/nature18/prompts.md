# nature18 prompts — DM for comment NATURE
Save as Claude Code commands (.claude/commands/<name>.md, "$ARGUMENTS" = your topic) or paste directly.

/time-lapse — Make a 9:16 full-screen 3D time-lapse film (three.js, rendered frame by frame with HTML + GSAP, ~4 s) of $ARGUMENTS.
Show it as a close side cutaway (behind glass where it helps), real materials and soft studio light, the camera close and slow.
Play the real stages in the right order, each one eased, one clear idea per shot. Labels with leader lines for each part, a short
caption word by word with one key word in a blue box. No invented numbers.
  Example: /time-lapse a seed sprouting  (soil cutaway: the bean seed coat splits → the root grows down first and branches →
  the shoot pushes up through the soil → two seed leaves open towards the light)

/explain-3d — Make a 9:16 full-screen 3D explainer (three.js, rendered frame by frame with HTML + GSAP, ~4 s) of $ARGUMENTS. The
camera stays close, the real process plays step by step, bloom for anything that glows. Labels + caption as above.
  Example: /explain-3d how lightning forms  (in a storm cloud ice crystals and drops collide → positive charge gathers at the top,
  negative at the bottom → a branching stepped leader zigzags down, a short streamer rises from a tree → they meet: the return
  stroke is the flash you see)

/zoom-in — Start wide on $ARGUMENTS in its real setting, then one slow eased camera push-in until the tiny structure fills the
frame, crisp and glinting (real materials, sparkle, bloom). Labels + caption as above.
  Example: /zoom-in a snowflake  (a flake lands on a dark wool mitten → push in to a six-armed hexagonal ice crystal → side
  branches that grow in cold, humid air)
