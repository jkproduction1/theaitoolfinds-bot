# made12 prompts — DM for comment MADE
Save as Claude Code commands (.claude/commands/<name>.md, "$ARGUMENTS" = your topic) or paste directly.

/how-its-made — Make a 9:16 full-screen 3D "how it's made" film (three.js, rendered frame by frame with HTML + GSAP, ~4 s) of
$ARGUMENTS, showing the real production steps in order, from raw materials to the finished product. Studio HDRI light, bloom,
depth. Labels with leader lines for each step, a short caption word by word with one key word in a blue box. No invented numbers.
  Example: /how-its-made a pencil  (graphite + clay pressed into rods and baked → laid in the grooves of a wood slat → a second slat
  glued on top → cut into pencils → painted)

/factory-line — Make a 9:16 full-screen 3D factory-line film (three.js, ~4 s) of $ARGUMENTS: follow one product through the real
machines, side view, see-through where the action happens inside, ending with finished products moving off on a conveyor.
Labels + caption as above.
  Example: /factory-line a Lego brick  (plastic granules → heated barrel melts them → a screw pushes the plastic into a steel mould
  → it cools, the mould opens, the brick pops out onto the belt)

/time-lapse — Make a 9:16 full-screen 3D time-lapse (three.js, ~4 s) of $ARGUMENTS from nothing to finished, in the real build
order. Slow camera move, the sky turns to dusk at the end and the lights come on. Labels + caption as above.
  Example: /time-lapse a tunnel being dug  (cross-section of the ground: a tunnel boring machine's cutter head spins and grinds the
  rock, a conveyor carries the dirt out, concrete rings line the tunnel behind it)
