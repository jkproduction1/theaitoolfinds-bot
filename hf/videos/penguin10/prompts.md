# 10 Claude animation styles — prompts (DM for "PENGUIN")

Base (put this first, then add one style line):
"Make a 9:16 looping animation as ONE self-contained HTML file, all drawn in code (canvas or three.js, no images).
A cute penguin with a red scarf walks left to right while the world scrolls past. Waddle walk cycle, scarf flutters
behind, ~1.2 s per loop, smooth easing, deterministic render(t) so I can screen-record it."

1. Pixel Waddle — "16-bit pixel art: render at low resolution and upscale with no smoothing, 1px dark outline, sunset sky in hard colour bands, pixel stars and snow."
2. Chalk Talk — "Chalk on a green blackboard: wobbly white strokes that boil at 8 fps, coloured chalk hatching for the scarf and beak, a chalk label 'scarf!' with an arrow."
3. Neon Line — "Synthwave neon: black background, glowing cyan outline penguin, pink scarf, magenta perspective grid floor scrolling, reflection under the penguin."
4. Clay Toy — "Plasticine 3D in three.js: soft matte materials with a subtle fingerprint bump, warm peach studio light, soft shadows, little clay trees and snow mounds."
5. Comic Pop — "Comic book: thick black outlines, flat colours, halftone dot shading, rotating yellow sunburst, speed lines and a 'WADDLE!' burst bubble."
6. Paper Cut — "Layered paper cut-out: parallax paper hills with soft drop shadows, paper sun, paper grain texture, every penguin part a separate cut-out with a shadow."
7. Blueprint — "Technical blueprint: blue grid paper, white line drawing, dashed construction circles, dimension arrows (height, stride), labels and a title block."
8. Ink Doodle — "Ink on cream paper: hand-inked wobbly outline, cross-hatched body, red watercolour wash for the scarf slightly off the lines, ink footprints behind."
9. Brick Toy — "Toy bricks in three.js: voxelise the penguin into small bricks with studs on top, glossy plastic, green studded baseplate, brick towers scrolling by."
10. Moonlight 3D — "Cinematic 3D night: full moon, falling snow particles, soft snow ground with footprints, cold rim light and a warm fill, glossy black body, fuzzy red scarf."
