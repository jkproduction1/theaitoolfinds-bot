## /explain-3d how a virus hijacks a cell
Make a 5-second 9:16 (1080x1920, 30 fps) three.js animation, camera close to a big cell.
- The cell surface fills the bottom: soft, wet, translucent pink membrane with small receptor stalks; darker organelles inside.
- A grey virus with red club-shaped spike proteins drifts in, docks on a receptor, the membrane dimples, wraps and swallows it.
- Inside, glowing green strands (the viral genes) spread; then new virus copies bud out of the membrane and drift away.
- Headline "The cell builds new viruses." Labels with a dot: "Spike proteins – lock onto the cell", "Viral genes – take over the cell",
  "New copies – leave to infect more". Slow push-in, then a gentle rise; nothing ever stands still.

## /zoom-in a mosquito bite
Make a 5-second 9:16 canvas animation: a skin cross-section up close (flaky top layer, epidermis cells, pink dermis with fibres, a hair,
a capillary with red blood cells flowing through).
- The mosquito's head comes in from the top (big compound eye, feathery antennae); its sheath (labium) lands on the skin, stays
  on the surface and buckles back; only the bundle of 6 thin needles goes in, probes left and right,
  puffs saliva (stops your blood clotting), finds the vessel, and blood runs up the straw. Slow zoom-in.
- Headline "6 needles in one bite." Labels: "6 needles – in one bundle", "Saliva – stops your blood clotting",
  "Labrum – finds a vessel, then sips". (Source: NPR/KQED 2016: 2 maxillae saw, 2 mandibles hold, the labrum finds the vessel and
  sips, the hypopharynx delivers saliva.)

## /x-ray the human spine
Make a 5-second 9:16 three.js animation in a blue x-ray look (additive fresnel glow, bloom, dark navy background, faint torso outline).
- A scan line sweeps down and reveals the column: 7 neck, 12 mid-back, 5 lower-back vertebrae with discs, then the fused sacrum and
  tailbone (33 bones in total). Then the column turns from the front to the side so its S-curve shows.
- Headline "33 bones in one column." Labels: "Neck – 7 vertebrae", "Mid back – 12 vertebrae", "Lower back – 5 vertebrae",
  "Sacrum – 5 fused", "Coccyx – 4 fused"; each region glows brighter when its label appears.
