Ever closed Claude Code and lost everything it learned about your project? Claude-Mem gives it a memory. It's a free, open source plugin. While you work, it records what Claude does, squeezes it into short summaries, and saves them on your machine. Next session, the right context shows up on its own. You can search your whole history in plain English. And anything inside private tags is never stored. There's a paid cloud, but you don't need it. Comment MEM and I'll send you the link.
