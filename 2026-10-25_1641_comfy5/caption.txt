Still clicking through menus in ComfyUI? These 5 shortcuts change that.

1. Double-click the canvas: search any node
2. Ctrl+B: bypass a node, wires pass through
3. Ctrl+M: mute a node
4. Ctrl+Enter: queue the graph (Ctrl+Shift+Enter puts it first)
5. Load an image made in ComfyUI: get its whole workflow and seed back

Free, open source (GPL-3.0) and runs offline. Cmd instead of Ctrl on Mac. Models are big downloads and a GPU helps a lot.
