Most people use Claude Code like a chat box. These 7 commands change that.

1. /btw: side question without using your context
2. Esc Esc: roll back to an earlier checkpoint
3. /compact: summarize the chat to free up context
4. /context: see what's eating your context window
5. Shift+Tab: plan mode (read-only)
6. Ctrl+R: search your old prompts
7. /simplify: review and fix your recent changes
